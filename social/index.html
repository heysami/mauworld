Mauworld overview

Mauworld is where Maumau becomes social.

It has a public world for dropping in, talking, reacting, browsing what is live, and joining nearby screen, video, voice, or game shares. When you sign in, Mauworld also unlocks persistent voice chat, private worlds, build mode, and tools for making a space feel like yours.

What everyday people can do here

Start simple, then go deeper only if you want to.

The code already supports the basic social stuff people actually reach for first: talking, finding what is live, showing someone a screen, keeping a voice room open, or moving a group into a private space.

Hang out in the public world

Join as a guest, pick a name, move around, bubble chat, and drop quick reactions without setting up a full account first.

Show someone what you are doing

Share a screen, camera feed, voice channel, or a game nearby, give it a title, and let people open it from the live list.

Keep a room for your own people

Signed-in users can open private worlds, share entry links, and keep a room, field, or board for friends, teammates, or a recurring group.

Find what is already active

Search live share titles, browse posts, and switch search modes to look up private worlds when you want to jump into something already happening.

Public World

The easy front door stays useful even before sign-in.

Public World is not just a splash screen. It already supports guest chat, reactions, live browsing, and search. That makes it a good first stop before you decide whether you want to share something, listen in, or move into a more private space.

Voice and live presence

Persistent voice is there when the room should stay open.

Signed-in users can start persistent voice chat and keep a live social layer around the world instead of treating every interaction like a one-off call.

Private Worlds

You can keep your own space instead of borrowing the public one.

Private worlds already support entry links, live feed activity, and different world types such as rooms, fields, and boards, so they can be casual hangouts or something more structured.

Build and AI tools

Mauworld can go beyond hanging out when you want to make something.

The private-world code already supports build mode, scenes, screens, models, textures, prefabs, scene logic, editor access, and AI-assisted screen HTML or scripts. For most people that means a space can start simple, then gradually become a custom place instead of staying a generic room forever.

Start here

Public World is still the best first click.

Enter it as a guest if you just want to look around. Sign in when you want nearby sharing, persistent voice, and private worlds to come with you.
