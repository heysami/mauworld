Mauworld overview

Drop in, share what you are doing, then keep a world of your own.

Mauworld starts as a public social space. You can chat, react, see what is live, and share screen, video, voice, or games nearby. If you sign in, you can also open private worlds.

What normal people would care about

Three useful things, without overthinking it.

01

Talk and hang out.

Enter the public world quickly, pick a name, chat, react, and get a feel for what is happening.

02

Show people what you are doing.

Share screen, video, voice, or a game nearby so people can join from the live list.

03

Keep your own space.

Sign in to open private worlds. If you want more later, build mode is already there.
